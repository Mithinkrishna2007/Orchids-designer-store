Introducing ORCHIDS

A designer store for Coimbatore.

Our boutique in KK Nagar, Sundakkamuthur serves clients from Kovaiputhur, Sundakkamuthur and across Coimbatore. Each piece on our shelves is chosen for its fabric, its finish and the way it wears.

We believe a beautiful outfit should feel personal. That is why ORCHIDS combines ready-to-wear designer collections with a custom stitching service built around your own ideas.

The ORCHIDS Boutique

Our Philosophy

Three things guide everything we do.

01

Designed with Care

Every saree, kurti and ensemble is presented with attention to quality, detail and enduring style.

02

Crafted with Care

Fabric, embroidery, borders and finishing matter to us. We look closely at the details, because that is where good craftsmanship shows.

03

Personally Yours

No two clients are the same. We listen to your preferences, your occasion and your ideas, and help you find or create something that feels like you.
