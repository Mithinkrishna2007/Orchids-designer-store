About ORCHIDS

A boutique built on personal attention.

ORCHIDS is a designer boutique in Coimbatore for women who care about how a garment is made. We curate designer sarees, premium kurtis and ethnic wear, and we offer personalised stitching so your outfit fits the way it should.

Every visit is unhurried. Whether you are choosing a saree for a wedding or bringing in a design you found online, the boutique team takes the time to understand what you want.
