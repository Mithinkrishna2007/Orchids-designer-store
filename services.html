Questions

Frequently asked.

Can I share a design I found online?

Yes. Send your reference photo or screenshot to ORCHIDS on WhatsApp and the boutique team will talk through your preferred style and stitching requirements with you.

Where is the ORCHIDS boutique?

ORCHIDS Designer Store is opposite Ashram School & ICICI Bank, KK Nagar, Sundakkamuthur, Coimbatore 641010, and serves clients from Kovaiputhur, Sundakkamuthur, KK Nagar and across Coimbatore.

How do I enquire about a product?

Every product on our Collections page has an “Enquire” button that opens WhatsApp with the product name already filled in. The boutique team will share details with you.

Can I buy online?

Our website is a showcase of the ORCHIDS collections. For availability and details, please message us on WhatsApp or visit the boutique.
